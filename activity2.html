Fact 4: About Spider Man

Spider-Man is a fictional superhero appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. He first appeared in Amazing Fantasy #15 (August 1962) and was created by writer Stan Lee and artist Steve Ditko. In his universe, Peter Parker is a shy and intelligent teenager who gains spider-like abilities after being bitten by a radioactive spider.

Spider-Man's character has become one of the most iconic and enduring superheroes in popular culture. He is known for his agility, strength, and web-slinging abilities, as well as his strong sense of responsibility and moral code. Spider -Man has been featured in numerous comic book series, animated television shows, and live-action films, making him a beloved character for fans of all ages.
